# NEXORA — Premium Demo Catalog Asset Brief

## Deliverable

Generate **240 modern technology products × 3 unique images = 720 images**.

This is a presentation-only catalog. Each visual must look like a premium, believable technology campaign image, but it must **not** be represented as an official manufacturer image.

### Non-negotiable image rules

- Each SKU has exactly three images: `01-hero`, `02-detail`, `03-rear-or-profile`.
- Every SKU must have a distinct silhouette, color/material combination, lighting balance and composition. Never reuse the same generated object for another SKU.
- The three images for one SKU must show the same fictional product design from different angles. They must not be three unrelated devices.
- The category must always be obvious: a phone looks like a phone, a laptop like a laptop, and so on.
- All assets are landscape **16:9**, at least **2048×1152**; the complete product is visible with at least 10% breathing room.
- No brand logo, model text, watermark, user interface, people, hands, boxes or random props.
- No distorted geometry: no duplicate camera lenses, melted bodies, impossible ports, floating keys or cropped object.
- Keep output naming exactly: `<category>/<slug>-01-hero.png`, `<slug>-02-detail.png`, `<slug>-03-profile.png`.

## Universal art direction

Use this base prompt for every image, then append the product and camera direction below.

```text
Use case: product-mockup
Asset type: NEXORA premium presentation catalog
Create a photorealistic, unbranded, modern [PRODUCT] for a luxury technology retailer.
The object must be fully visible, physically plausible and unique from every other product in this catalog.

Environment: deep charcoal seamless studio, refined warm-orange rim light,
subtle cool-blue reflection, soft natural contact shadow, restrained cinematic haze.
Style: high-end campaign product photography, realistic premium materials, clean composition.
Frame: landscape 16:9, whole product visible with 10% clear space around it.
Avoid: logo, lettering, watermark, UI, packaging, hands, people, extra objects,
cropping, duplicate parts, distorted geometry, impossible ports or screens.
```

### The three camera directions

1. **01 hero** — three-quarter front view, full product, hero composition, product slightly right of centre.
2. **02 detail** — a close but complete, recognisable material/feature view; screen, camera array, keyboard, hinge, earcup, lens or texture depending on category.
3. **03 profile** — rear three-quarter, side profile or a low sculptural angle; the whole device must still be visible.

## Generation schedule

1. Generate 10 SKUs first (30 images) and visually approve uniqueness.
2. Generate in category batches of 10 SKUs (30 images), never one giant batch.
3. Name, inspect and reject any duplicate-looking object before starting the next category.
4. Do not overwrite original images; save new files in `static/generated-demo/` first.
5. Only after visual QA, connect an approved 3-image group to its matching Django product.

## Quality checklist for every generated group

- Same object across image 01/02/03? Yes.
- Correct category immediately recognisable? Yes.
- Product entirely visible in image 01 and 03? Yes.
- Different composition from every other SKU? Yes.
- No text/logo/watermark/people? Yes.
- Premium studio lighting and clean geometry? Yes.
- File name matches its SKU and camera role? Yes.
